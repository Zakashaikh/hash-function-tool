ℹ️ About Hash Functions

MD5

128-bit hash function. Fast but cryptographically broken. Used for checksums.

SHA-1

160-bit hash function. Deprecated for security applications due to vulnerabilities.

SHA-256

256-bit hash function. Currently secure and widely used in blockchain and security.

SHA-512

512-bit hash function. More secure than SHA-256, used for high-security applications.
